# What is Yoko

Yoko is a self-management app. It keeps your recurring actions, events, tasks, notes and mood in one place, so small habits and important dates don't get lost across calendars, to-do apps and notes.

Everything works without an account, saved in your browser. Once the back-end is ready, signing in will keep your data long term.

**[Try it at yoko.arualvivan.com](https://yoko.arualvivan.com)**

## Features

Available:

- **Recurring actions:** track something you do (or stop doing) and count the days. For example, how long since you stopped eating junk food.
- **Events:** keep important dates and count down to them. For example, a friend's graduation.
- **Calendar:** see every action and event in one view.

Planned:

- **Tasks:** plan your day with time ranges and statuses.
- **Notes:** write things down.
- **Mood:** register how you feel and look back at your history.
- **Greeting by period of the day:** the header follows sunrise and sunset where you are.
- **Account:** sign in to keep your data and sync what you saved before signing in.

The rules behind each feature are in the [product requirements](https://github.com/lauvivans/yoko/blob/develop/docs/product/requirements.md).
