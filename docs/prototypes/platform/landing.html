Places

Remember every place you’ve been

A map of your own, with the photos and stories of each place.

  • Pin it on the map. Tap where you went and the pin drops right there.
  • Keep the photos. Every place has its own gallery.
  • Sort by category. Travel, social, hobby, reading or family, each with its own color.
  • Favorite the best ones. So they’re easy to find when a friend asks for tips.
  • Write what to remember. When you went and a note for next time.
  • Find it again. Search by name or neighborhood, and pan and zoom around the map.
