Your days, in one place.

Yoko keeps your events, recurring actions, tasks, notes, places and links together, and asks how you’re feeling along the way.

Start for free See what it does

Everything you want to keep track of

  • Occurrences

    Count down to your events and count up the days you kept your habits, with goals.

  • Places by Nori

    Pin the places you’ve been to on a map and keep their photos.

  • Links

    Save what you find on the web, sort it into collections and track what you’ve read.

  • Tasks

    Plan your day with time ranges and a status for each task.

  • Notes

    Write down whatever is on your mind, with or without a title.

  • Mood

    Tell Yoko how you feel and look back at your mood history.

Open source, made in public

Yoko is a side project built in the open. The code, the decisions and this prototype are all on GitHub, and your data can be exported whenever you want.
